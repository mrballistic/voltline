# AI assistance

Voltline was built with AI assistance, and this page says where.

- **Design, code, tests and docs** were produced with Anthropic's Claude, working through Claude Code, under Todd Greco's direction and review.
- **Piece art and the 404 page** are drawn in code, from mockups that were made the same way.
- **The in-game AI and the hints** are a deterministic search engine, not generative AI. They run on your device and make no network calls.
- **Sound effects** were chosen by Todd and by Claude from Freesound packs, all CC0 or CC BY. Per-file provenance is in `audio-src/voltline-sounds.xlsx`, and attribution is in [CREDITS.md](./CREDITS.md).
