Tap targets

44 × 44 minimum on every control, phone and desktop: buttons, the mute button, the speed segments, the AI switch row and the volume slider’s hit area. Footer links get a 28 px tall target (above the 24 px floor in WCAG 2.5.8).

Cost: none on the phone (the cell stays 45, limited by width). On a 900 px tall desktop the cell drops from 56 to 53. If you want 56 back, use 40 px controls under (pointer: fine) only.

Focus ring

New game Got it

2 px solid #ffe14d, 3 px offset, following each control’s radius, on :focus-visible only. 15.2:1 on the page, 13.0:1 on dialogs, and unlike any control’s own color, so it never blends into a cyan or magenta button.

Turn off MUI’s focus ripple (disableRipple on ButtonBase) so the ring is the only focus signal.

Disabled treatment

Hint Hint

Left: today, about 2:1. Right: text #7d86b8 at 5.6:1, a dashed 1 px border, no fill, no glow. The dashed edge says “off” without relying on color, and the label stays readable.

Apply it to every disabled control: Hint, the AI switch and the speed toggle during graphics failure, and the muted volume slider.
