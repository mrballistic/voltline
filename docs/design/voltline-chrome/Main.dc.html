How to play

The goal

Swap two neighboring pieces to line up three or more of the same color. They clear, new pieces drop in, and every match that follows chains for more points. A swap that makes no match slides back.

Special pieces

Charge. Made by matching 4 in a line. Clears the 3×3 area around it.

Arc. Made by an L or T shape. Clears its whole row and column.

Prism. Made by matching 5 in a line. Swap it with a colored piece to clear every piece of that color (rocks stay).

Specials chain: an explosion that reaches another special sets it off.

The tray

Every 10 pieces of one color you clear earns a spare piece of that color. The tray holds five, and counting pauses while it’s full, so spend one. Tap a tray piece, then a plain piece on the board (or drag it there) to swap it in. Placing costs a move.

Rocks

From level 5, gray rocks start appearing. Clear them by matching three, or with an explosion. A Prism swapped with a rock clears every rock on the board.

Levels and game over

Fill the progress bar to level up. Past level 8, refills get harder. The game ends when no move can make a match, tray placements included, so a spare piece can save you.

Hint and AI mode

Hint shows the best moves as arrows.

AI mode plays for you until you switch it off.

Controls

Tap or click a piece, then a neighbor. Or drag (or swipe) a piece toward a neighbor.
